How I approach SEO content

I treat each article as part of a larger content system rather than an isolated writing assignment.

1. Start with the reader's problem. Define who is searching, what they are trying to do, and what is making the task difficult.
2. Check how the page fits the site. Review existing content, search intent, topic overlap, internal links, and whether the page should be new or updated.
3. Understand the product before writing. Check product documentation, current features, use cases, screenshots, claims, and the exact role the product should play in the article.
4. Build the article around useful answers. Use direct explanations, examples, workflows, comparison points, and clear section structure instead of writing around a keyword repeatedly.
5. Review the system around the article. Check internal links, CTAs, metadata, supporting visuals, publishing priorities, and which older pages should link back to it.
