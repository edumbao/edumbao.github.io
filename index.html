About

I learned SEO by publishing first, long before I worked inside a SaaS content team.

My path into SEO has always been practical.

I started working online through my own websites and learned search by trying to understand why some pages were discovered and others were not.

That eventually led me into professional SEO and content work for companies such as Outscraper and Targetron , where the job became much broader than writing individual blog posts.

Today, much of my work connects search intent, customer problems, product capabilities, internal links, analytics, and publishing priorities. I also keep learning the technical side by building small tools and working directly with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, APIs, and newer web workflows.
