03 / Documentation

I want the notes to be as useful as the build.

Each project gives me something technical to practice, but it also gives me a technical-writing exercise. I use the same questions to keep the documentation honest.

  1. 01 What I tried
  2. 02 Why I tried it
  3. 03 What worked
  4. 04 What broke or confused me
  5. 05 What I learned
  6. 06 How it connects to SEO, content, APIs, or documentation
  7. 07 What I will try next

Some projects will become useful tools. Some will stay experiments. Failed attempts still belong here if they taught me something worth documenting.
